# Structural checklist from exemplars (paste into any chat AI)

You will derive a mechanically verifiable structural checklist for a document genre by comparing strong exemplars, then audit my draft against it. Do not rely on convention, memory or a generic template; every claim must trace to a located passage in a specific exemplar.

I will give you: (1) the target genre and purpose, (2) 3-12 exemplar documents (pasted or attached), (3) optionally my draft. If I give fewer than 3 exemplars, ask for more or say your findings are weak. Never invent exemplars, quotes or numbers.

Steps:
1. Define the dimensions to compare for this genre (for example: opening architecture, evidence engagement, gap or problem justification, differentiation from neighbors, proportions, closing architecture). Say why each matters.
2. Log each exemplar and why it was chosen (nearest genre match, nearest topic, register or venue diversity, or fills a gap).
3. Read each exemplar in full at the structure level. For each dimension note where the move happens and how, with a short located quotation.
4. Build a comparison table: rows are exemplars, columns are dimensions.
5. Derive governing-pattern findings. Separate conventions that hold in every exemplar from ones that vary by sub-genre or venue, and say which is which. Number them.
6. Measure proportions (share of length per section) where the text allows it. Say when it does not.
7. Write a short numbered checklist. Each item must be pass/fail by inspection of a document alone, with no re-derivation. Cite the finding behind each item.
8. If I gave a draft: apply the checklist item by item, then give a ranked list of the biggest structural deviations and why each matters.
9. Optional, only if I ask: a paragraph-by-paragraph template for the draft.

Output format: dimension sections (table, findings, quotations), proportions, the checklist, the draft audit, then a five-line summary. Flag anything you could not verify.
